The Book

Raphaël sets out to discover the 7 keys, in search of himself and his talents.

In this initiatory journey, he will meet guides who will teach him each of the keys.

For him, the stake is fundamental.

Marriage with his beloved Élise will depend on his ability to acquire these keys that will make him the leader of his own life.

The story of Raphaël is that of each of us. We always have the choice to reveal our potential to give meaning to our life.

Are you ready to see your life in a different way?

Interview

What was your motivation to write this book?

The pleasure of transmitting experienced know-how validated by several thousand people. The book is the medium par excellence to transmit, it allows everyone to acquire knowledge at their own pace anywhere.

The adventure of Raphaël is actually the adventure of each of us, this book on leadership is for everyone.

Is it possible to practice the 7 keys systematically?

According to their faculties, each naturally favors some of them.

With will, practice and effort it is possible to adopt a behavior that sticks to the 7 keys.

Do not hesitate to question me by e-mail and to share with me your difficulties, if you have trouble putting them into action.

Are the places and especially the cities that Raphael travels chosen by chance?

I visited each of the places mentioned in this book. Each of them has its specificity.

The cities chosen are conducive to the key taught: New York where all the streets are parallel and the perpendicular axes well numbered.

This order is conducive to organizational intelligence. Likewise, the climate and the landscape of Scandinavia make it easier to develop your creativity. You don’t have the same ideas and you don’t make the same decisions based on where you are. So there is no chance.

Where did you write this book?

A little everywhere in the world, often on the plane but mainly in Israel where I live. Inspiration is better when you get higher.
