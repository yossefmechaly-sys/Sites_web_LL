Reflexion & Vision

Earth

What lives is round. Our planet is a work that transcends human reason. Since the 70 nations moved away from Babylon nearly 5,000 years ago, each chose the place where they could fulfill their role. For humanity to move in the right direction, it is essential that every nation fulfill its role. This task can only be performed when the person resides on the land that suits him. The Talmud teaches us that ” for as a human, so the tree of the field.” Just as in order for a tree to bear the best fruit, it must be planted in the ground that makes it fertile, with the climate, the amount of sun and water needed for its flowering; To give his best, the person needs to be connected to a place where he can reveal his talents. The place where he will settle down to raise his family and grow himself is unique to everyone. When the fruit is excellent, it means that the tree is planted in the right place. When the individual succeeds, it means that he has found his place.

The earth is a bubble of man. Every organ plays a vital role for man to live; if the eye fulfills its function to capture the light, if the heart beats properly so that blood flows, if the lungs provide the oxygen needed for all our organs and so on, then the person will be healthy. Is the role of water more important than that of wood? Is the kidney more important than the ear?

What facilitates the person’s realization is the variety of organs that make them up. Everyone must fulfill their role. The harmony between all organs that allows a person to live in good health. No organ is more important than any other organ. If the brain is functioning well and only one cell is causing the tumor, then there the proper functioning of the entire metabolism is impaired.

The planet needs everyone to do their part. No nation can do without another nation. An imbalance between nations in the world causes problems, whether economic, ecological, political or military, on the other side of the world. I have traveled extensively around the world and visited over fifty countries. Every region is magnificent. Every city, country is amazing, each one different from the other and the variety of landscapes, cultures, skin colors is what makes our world unique.

It is necessary more than ever to find the bridge that will allow us to get closer to each other, and then discover that what separates us is actually a force that allows the world to evolve in the right direction. The unification of the 70 nations, while respecting the uniqueness of each of them and while allowing them to flourish in their individuality, is part of the desire of each and every one. For me, this is a must.
